FRIED CHICKEN

Ingredient Chicken: 1 kg bone-in chicken pieces (drumsticks, thighs, or breasts)Brine / Wet Marinade:1 cup buttermilk (or regular milk mixed with 1 tbsp lemon juice)1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp hot sauce or mild chili paste1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepperDry Coating Mix:2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)1/4 cup cornstarch (cornflour) for extra crunch1 tsp paprika or Kashmiri chili powder1/2 tsp garlic powder and 1/2 tsp onion powder1/2 tsp dried oregano or mixed herbs Salt and pepper to tasteFrying Oil: Vegetable or canola oil for deep frying.
