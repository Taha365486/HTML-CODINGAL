Tandur Chicken

In Indian Food Legend

Tandoori chicken is one of the world's most popular Indian culinary exports, renowned for its signature smoky aroma and bright red appearance.

Full Name:Kundan Lal Gujral

Position:Restauretior and Chef

Nationallity:Indian

Known For:Inventing and popularizing tandoori chicken, as well as butter chicken (murgh makhni) and dal makhani in the late 1940s after moving to New Delhi.
