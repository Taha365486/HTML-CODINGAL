Top 10 Food In India

Biryani

: A fragrant, slow-cooked layered rice dish mixed with aromatic spices and meat (chicken or mutton) or vegetables.

Dosa

A thin, crispy South Indian rice and lentil crepe filled with spiced mashed potatoes, served with sambar and coconut chutney.

Butter Chicken

: Tender pieces of yogurt-marinated chicken cooked in a rich, velvety, mildly spiced tomato and butter sauce (makhani).

Dal Makhani

Whole black lentils and red kidney beans slow-cooked with generous amounts of butter and cream.

Pani Puri

Crisp hollow spheres filled with tangy tamarind water, potatoes, and spices, leading India's legendary street food scene.

Wada Pav

the ultimate street food icon of Mumbai and Maharashtra, often called the "Indian burger" or the "poor man's burger."

Nan Roti

A soft, tandoor-baked flatbread brushed with butter or garlic.

Samosa

India's most beloved tea-time snack, recognizable by its iconic crispy, golden-brown triangular pastry shell.

Chole Bature

A classic North Indian dish pairing spicy chickpea curry with fluffy, deep-fried bread.

Gulab Jamun

Deep-fried milk-solid dough balls soaked in a sticky, rose-scented sugar syrup.
